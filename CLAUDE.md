# Shift: working rules

- Read `docs/CONCEPT.md` first in every new session. It is the handoff: what
  Shift is, why, the sources, every decision in the author's words, the status
  and the next step. Then `docs/PLAN.md` (the design) and `docs/SPEC.md` (the
  Supervisor track). Do not re-derive any of it from chat.
- This is a training tool with game mechanics, not a video game. Its output is
  practice, never an assessment or a readiness input.
- Built with the HeirsOfRuin game-building skills (`Game-Workshop/skills/`).
  Read `game-building/SKILL.md` before writing game logic. Follow its stages
  and gates: spec, kill gate, design doc, engine, calibration, slice, feature
  loop. Skipping a stage is allowed; skipping it silently is not.
- No engine and no art before the kill gate (`prototypes/killgate/`) passes
  and the author has seen its numbers.
- Content is fiction. No real person, no real order, nothing that identifies
  the author's employer, and no text from the author's assessment item bank in
  this repository.
- Decisions go in a dated table with the author's words quoted. Chat does not
  survive the session.
- Scratch files are `tools/_*.mjs` or `*.tmp.*` and are never committed.
  `dist/`, `out/`, `release/` are output and never committed.
- The repository is private. Do not make it public or open a pull request
  unless the author asks.
- Write for a reader who was not there: plain words, the failure behind each
  rule, the number that was measured.
