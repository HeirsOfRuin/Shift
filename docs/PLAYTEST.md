# Shift: a note for testers

*To send with the link. Written 2026-11-04 for the first group of leads and
supervisors. Plain words on purpose; the author edits it before it goes.*

---

You have a link to a thing called Shift. It is a practice tool, not a test.
It runs on your phone, in the browser, and takes about twenty minutes a go.

**What it is.** A pretend car plant called Northern Motors. You run the
welding department, or you are the lead hand, or the manager above, your
choice. Each module is a few weeks of that job: a crew with problems you can
see, causes you cannot, forty hours a week, and a paint department that
wants six bodies. You decide what to spend your hours on. Friday tells you
what happened. Some of it lands weeks later.

**What it is for.** Practice, and a conversation after. Nobody is scored.
Nothing you do in it goes anywhere unless you send it, and what you send is
what you typed, not your runs.

**What to do.**

0. If you were sent a code with the link (it starts with SHFTA1.), tap
   *Paste a code* on the first screen and paste it. Your modules are set by
   whoever sent it; skip to step 2.
1. Open the link. Tap *Start: who are you here?* and fill in the profile:
   your seat, your department, what you would call a strength, what you
   would want to work on. Two minutes. Be honest; it only decides the order
   of the modules.
2. Play the first module on your list. Read the brief, run the weeks, read
   Friday each time. When it ends, read the report. About twenty minutes.
3. Tap *Menu*, then *Your thoughts, for the coach*. Four questions: what felt
   true to your floor, what felt fake, what was missing, anything else. One
   line each is plenty. Tap *Send*, or copy the text and message it.
4. If you have another twenty minutes, play it again. It draws new causes.
5. If you came in on a code, tap *Progress code, for your coach* on your
   track when you are done, and send it the same way as your thoughts.

**Three questions, for the version from 2026-11-06 on.** Did you ever doubt
what someone in the game told you, and why? When the end screen told you
what the real reason was, were you surprised? Which part of a ticket did you
skim?

**What we most want to know.** Which bits would never happen on a real floor.
Which option you wanted and could not find. Whether the Friday report sounds
like a person or a robot. How long it took.

**The small print.** Your profile and your runs live on your phone and
nowhere else. If you switch phones, *Copy a save code* in the menu moves them.
This is a beta: if a screen does something odd, that is worth telling us too.
